confirmed silent *for Rome* — no movement, no battle, no treasury change. The "Rome's
only reachable path onto an enemy army tile is the adjacent attack click" inference in
the draft holds against this observation.

#### Claim 8 — chase ghost: memory scan followed a stale record [confirmed]

Across b3a_AUTO0733–0738 (six AI turns), Rome id=0 stays pinned at (93, 28) the entire
run; the real Gaul id=2 walks from (120, 54) toward Locri (capturing Tarrentum on the
way, depleting 25923→17414 from attrition/sieges), and Gaul id=8 marches from (72, 30)
down to (93, 34) capturing Locri (week 5 0736) and sieging Rhegium twice (weeks 7 and 9,
both `fails to capture`). AUTO0738 news adds the second AI-AI army destruction in this
batch: *"Illyria destroys army of Greece."* The capture pattern matches what a stale
memory record chasing a non-existent Gaul would predict: Rome's driver walked Rome to
(93, 28) where no army sits, while the real Gaul does its own thing in the south. Draft
diagnosis correct.

## Notes for the player (none blocking)

1. **The draft's "Three natural observations" claim should arguably be four.** AUTO0723's
   news has *"Seleucid destroys army of Galatia."* (week 7). The Galatia id=12 (194, 46)
   between AUTO0721→AUTO0722 is *not* that destruction (it's a Galatia-side merge like
   Media's; see claim 2b), but the 0722→0723 Galatia destruction is a real AI-AI army
   destruction the draft doesn't itemise. Total AI-AI army destructions in the b1 control
   therefore: Carthage→Celtiberia (week 3), Seleucid→Bithynia (week 5, mid-walk),
   Seleucid→Ptolemaic (week 1, news only, masked in army diff), **Seleucid→Galatia
   (week 7, news-only — the corresponding army record is presumably masked by an id
   churn in AUTO0722's "(188, 44)" Galatia 19 300-troop army)**. The "What this does
   not establish" § already notes the news form is masked in the army diff.
2. **The staged onto-click is brittle to compare directly**: there is one full AI turn
   between the staged input and the SAV that captures the result, and during that turn
   Gaul walked. The intake therefore confirms "the onto-click did not change Rome's state
   during the turn in which it was issued", not "Gaul stood still". The draft already
   presents the no-op for Rome's state, so this is consistent.
3. **"+38 within-turn growth"** in claim 2 is an inference (Seleucid's economy phase),
   not a separately-snapshotted value. Not a blocker; could be tightened by quoting the
   nation-treasury field at start-of-AI-turn vs end-of-AI-turn.
