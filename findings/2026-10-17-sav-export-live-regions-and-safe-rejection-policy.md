# SAV export: live nation regions, constructor residue and a conservative rejection policy

2026-10-17. **Draft for independent intake; [confirmed, partial] source/public-save/original-x86 evidence.** Research [#37](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/issues/37), latest [writer-gap comment](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/issues/37#issuecomment-6239985304); conquest [#99](https://github.com/diegoami/ic2-conquest/issues/99). Codex/OpenAI implements; owner selected MiniMax M3 for independent review.

**Result:** nation `+048..2E3` is a live sorted owned-city index list, not an unnamed static DAT block. `+434/+43C/+448` are historical 270-BC comparison baselines, not current-state duplicates. `+44E` is the live/history conqueror field. Most preferences/control bytes are named below, but **31 bytes remain semantically unresolved**: `+450..46A`, `+46D`, `+491..493`. They are explicitly **UNKNOWN / reject changed-state inheritance**, even though all sampled bytes are zero. Record construction leaves residue: an original new army initializes only **96/656 bytes** and a fleet construction order **10/26**. Relocation copies the whole record and can require carrier-index repair.

This is a byte-complete classification and **minimal safe rejection policy**, not a completed semantic exporter or original-compatibility approval. Missing meanings, missing native history/fields, new-record residue policy and the three required Windows round trips remain gates. In particular, the twelve owner JSON saves have not been inspected or converted here; their convertibility is not established. No owner files, generated SAVs, remake code, original fixtures or task #25 work were changed.

### Exact city-list and baseline evidence

`FUN_0044B8F4`, listing **50051–50101**, removes the transferred city from the old owner's list, shifts entries, writes -1 at the new end and inserts the city into the new owner's list by original string comparison (`00405C08`). The helper does **not** update owners/counts itself: its callers own that surrounding transaction. Our control verifies the helper's list effects with the original counts, rather than calling this a complete city-transfer simulation.

`TFindCity_ChangeCity` (`0043D93C`, 41564–41599) consumes list indices for display. Recruitment-city selection `FUN_004502E0` (53626–53729) walks this list, stopping on -1 or its original index limit. The pinned DAT profile initializes unused entries to -1; the observed public active prefixes and inactive entries agree in all 224 records. A canonical derived list must preserve that profile invariant; it cannot mix the native owner map with unrelated list bytes.

DAT loader `004481A0` reads current population `+430`, then copies it to `+434`; reads treasury `+438`, copies it to `+43C`; reads count `+446`, copies it to `+448`. Those copies are initialization, **not load-time recalculation**: the SAV loader reads the complete saved nation bytes as-is. `THumanFalls_InitializeForm @00455E38` **56436–56448** displays the saved baselines under the **270 BC** column, alongside current values at 56461–56473. A changed native export must not replace the start column with today's numbers. Source profile applicability requires the actual scenario/history, not merely 16 nations/334 cities or a matching title string.

Conqueror `+44E` is written at 50384 (`0044BED8`), 50535 (`0044C360`, -1), 50739 (`0044C528`), and read at 56395–56407 for the conquest/deposition explanation. This is evidence of meaning, not a complete inference rule from arbitrary normalized native state.

## Army/fleet records, prototypes and relocation

| Area | Established evidence | Minimal policy |
|---|---|---|
| Army header `+00..0F` | Eight WORDs: X/Y, owner, moves, covered-cell (-1 embarked), supplies, purse, morale. Constructor `00449F08` **48713–48752** initializes these. | Map every field, including covered-cell/embarkation state, with original bounds/units. No inherited purse/morale/movement from an unrelated army. |
| Twenty army unit slots, `+10+32*k` | Origin, type, troops, quality (four WORDs), then 24-byte C-string buffer. Constructor writes only type/troops=0; it leaves origin, quality and names. Mobilization `0044A4E0` explicitly supplies live slot values and a generated name. | Live slots require all typed values and a valid bounded name. Troops=0 does not prove the rest of a slot is byte-zero. Inactive quality/name/origin residue is not a new live prototype. |
| New army | Original-x86 `0xA5` control: **96 written bytes, 560 retained**. Counter cap 198 rejects creation, verified separately. | **No whole-record prototype inheritance.** A new-record policy must specify all live fields and justify inactive/text residue handling; that complete policy is not proven here. Reject missing fields/residue policy. Synthetic `0xA5` is never an export prototype. |
| Fleet: 13 WORDs, 26 bytes | X/Y, destination X/Y, owner, construction countdown, moves, supplies, purse, ships, condition **or building city**, carried army index, sea-cell/weather state. Constructors/consumers distinguish launched/building contexts. | Every context-dependent live field must be mapped. Building-city is not condition; uninitialized building coordinates are not a launched-fleet prototype. |
| Fleet construction | `0044A004` **48753–48771** assigns owner/countdown=24/ships/build-city/carrier=-1 and charges ships×10; control writes **10 bytes, leaves 16**. Completion `0044A050` **48772–48799** sets launch position, condition=100, countdown=-1, carrier=-1, supplies=50, purse=0 and sea-cell=0. | Do not reuse a construction record as a ready fleet without all completion/state fields. No proof that a new native fleet's omitted destinations/moves may inherit residue. |
| Tombstones | `0044AB90` changes army owner to -1, restores underlay or detaches carrier; other 654 record bytes remain. `0044AD38` marks fleet owner -1 and may delete its carried army. | Tombstone is a state marker, not a zero record. Preserve exact bound tombstones for identity; reject invented/unmapped records rather than blanking them. |
| Army relocation | `0044ABE0` copies the entire last army (656 bytes) to a removed slot, updates every matching fleet carrier index and decrements count. | Preserve original record order when possible. Relocation needs a complete native→original index map and every carrier reference fixed; copy each record's own residue, not destination residue. |
| Fleet relocation | `0044AD7C` copies all 26 last-record bytes and decrements count. `0044ADB0` compacts tombstones descending. | Preserve whole-record lineage. A saved army has an embarkation sentinel/coordinates, not a fleet-pointer field to invent. |
| Unit-slot relocation | `0044AC3C`/`0044ACB4` copy whole 32-byte slots; removing a last slot clears troops only. | Do not treat normalized live-unit lists as an original byte envelope; retain identity/order and know when inactive fields matter. |

The new-record conclusion is deliberately a rejection boundary, not a promise that no safe constructor can ever be implemented. It shows what a complete typed mapper must supply and why constructor residue is insufficient evidence for either a whole-record template or a whole-record zero default. Canonical inactive/text padding, all reachable reads and a complete new-record profile still need justification before enabling native-only new records.

## Indices, map representations, text and pending state

- **No serialized CPU pointers in the named strategic record fields.** Counts and array indices remain indices. `00449920`/`00449970` scan coordinates and return the **first** live army/fleet match; the two control cases verify index 0, then index 1 after tombstoning 0. This differs from tactical slot-selection rules. Do not sort units arbitrarily or invent stable IDs from markers; reject ambiguous newly synthesized overlaps unless ordering/underlay/carrier semantics are explicitly preserved.
- **Map codes are owner + size band, not record indices.** `0044A80C` gives army bases 200/216/232 below 25,000 / below 50,000 / otherwise; `0044A878` gives fleet 300/316/332 below 25 / below 50 / otherwise. Boundary controls exercise 24,999/25,000/49,999/50,000 and 24/25/49/50. The native owner, totals, location, covered cell and carrier linkage must agree with the marker/underlay. Current weather/rough sea and static original terrain are not interchangeable. Missing underlay, contradictory stacking or unmapped terrain rejects; these controls are **not a full canonical map-rebuilder**.
- **Text is single-byte/NUL, not UTF-8.** Nation/city/leader/unit/news capacities are **11/14/27/24/61 bytes**, including the NUL, hence supported ASCII maxima **10/13/26/23/60**. Reject embedded NUL, unmappable/non-ASCII text in the minimal profile, overlong strings, and text that overflows any original assembled message buffer. Exact ANSI behavior for new non-ASCII owner text is not established. Validate generated message lengths too; the news writer is unbounded `StrCopy`, so a field fitting its own buffer is not a blanket guarantee for concatenated messages.
- **Residue is real.** Original `StrCopy @00405B00` writes prefix+NUL, leaving the rest; the bounded synthetic test writes 3 bytes for `Hi` and retains 29 bytes. Whole-record/slot/news copies retain residue. Byte-identity requires the same authentic buffer; a renamed existing bound buffer can reproduce prefix+NUL writes, but normalized text alone cannot recover historic trailing bytes. No byte-identical recovery claim is made.
- **News framing and pending offers stay distinct.** Reuse the corrected news report: newest slot index -1..39; 0..39 full 61-byte slots; shift register, no circular head pointer. The separate trailer's two WORDs are offer source/type. `00452034` resets source to -1 at a new human turn; `0045AC5C` **58212–58228** re-announces a saved offer on load when the relationship differs. Acceptance does not necessarily clear the block; relation state suppresses repeat dialog. Missing pending-offer history must reject faithful export, not silently clear or reroll it. If source=-1 the stale type remains possible; do not infer it is meaningful or byte-zero.
- **Turn/controller consistency is live.** The sixteen-entry permutation, current nation, seat index, calendar and all human flags must map consistently. Do not call New Game's random order generator as a serialization default. Loader `004487C4` resets runtime selected army/fleet indices to -1; those selections are not a saved pointer map. UI geometry is saved separately from calendar.
- **Pause/general/save flags are not one field.** Nation `+48C/+48E` are saved battle delays and `+490` is controller. The strategic saved battle flag gates the conditional tactical block. Native states with an open battle or unsupported paused/mid-resolution/modal state are rejected by this minimal strategic policy; this does not revive the stale claim that tactical saves are impossible. Original runtime general flags and the save/quit flag at `004A0334` are not in the strategic trailer. Existing tactical evidence documents same-process transient behavior; this investigation does not reconstruct it or claim RNG equivalence.

## Minimal safe supported-state / rejection contract

1. **Unchanged authentic identity:** copying the exact bound original envelope is a separate supported identity path only when the complete relevant state is unchanged and framing/version validation passes. A public reader's empty unmapped-field list or normalized equality is insufficient. The four private T184 identity templates reported in [PR1202](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial_conquest_2/pull/1202) are that author's evidence; they were not supplied to or retested here and do not prove changed-state inheritance.
2. **Changed existing state:** require explicit native→original mapping for every live field and reference, exact unit/calendar/economy/controller semantics, faithful scenario/history applicability for canonical fields, a complete record lineage/mutation plan, and proved conservation of every preserved region. **This packet leaves 31 nation bytes and new-record residue semantics unresolved: changed-state export remains unsupported where those dependencies cannot be established.** A bound template alone does not remove that gate.
3. **Native-only / new / relocated objects:** no envelope is recreated by serializing known values over an unrelated template. Relocation is whole-record lineage plus index repair, not a prototype. New records need a fully justified field/default/residue policy; missing information rejects. The twelve owner saves' empty provenance does not become valid original provenance merely because they name the faithful scenario.
4. **Fail closed with a specific reason:** unknown region dependency, missing original history, unsupported active/modal/tactical state or unrepresented pending offer, lost record residue/identity needed by the chosen preservation path, incompatible text, inconsistent counts/indices/carrier/map/underlay or unproved controller/calendar mapping. No arbitrary zero-fill, silent offer clearing, RNG stream regeneration or future-behavior claim.
5. **Only after an applicable changed-state profile is implemented:** obtain candidates and private inputs through owner coordination, preserve every original/hash, then perform the independent native Windows gate below. Self-reader round trips are framing checks, not this gate.
