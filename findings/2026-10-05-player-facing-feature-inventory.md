# Player-facing feature inventory of the original (battle excluded)

**Status:** draft finding from `ic2-conquest` (branch `experiment/feature-inventory`), awaiting promotion. Task: `docs/tasks/feature-inventory.md`. **Wine-only where a run is cited:** the runs are the original `Imperial Conquest 2 fast rollingsave seed.exe` under Wine 9.0; nothing here was seen on a desktop Windows install. The clone (`imperial_conquest_2`) was not available on this machine and was not read.

## Answer

- **146 feature rows**, in the groups the task names, each with a tag earned by its cited evidence: **86 [confirmed]** (a save, a screenshot or a `coverage.md` row) and **60 [derived]** (a function, a form, a help topic or a report section); **no [candidate]**. No row is inferred from the clone; inferences are in their own section.
- **Coverage:** every source entry (UI entries, message families and a rules-and-events family swept from the rules digest, `coverage.md` §4 and the report titles) is mapped to a row or to a stated exclusion, **0 unaccounted**, recomputed from tracked inputs by `runs/experiments/feature_inventory/coverage_check.py` (totals below, versioned output tracked, self-test included).
- **Every unwired item of the clone has an original row** (table "The clone's unwired items"); none is "no such feature in the original".
- **Four things the sources disagree on or hide**, found on the way: the `TAFSupply` form (the Supply army / Supply fleet dialog, which the task's `TAF*` exclusion would have dropped, and which has a **Buy supplies** button); the About box's misspelled **CAncell** button opens a **Cellular Automata** window (seen in Wine); the shortcut for the leader's capital is **Ctrl+Q** in the code and in Wine, **Ctrl+P** in the help file; and **Split army** reuses the army-to-army transfer form.

## Method

- **Extraction (scripts in `runs/experiments/feature_inventory/`, outputs in `runs/experiments/data/run-exp-feature-inventory/`).**
  - `extract_forms.py`: reads every `TPF0` form resource of `Imperial Conquest 2.exe` (29 forms, 908 controls: `forms.json`, `form_controls.tsv` with captions, hints, event handlers and Delphi `ShortCut` values decoded to `Shift+W` etc.). The `TMainMenu` stream is inside `TPremierForm`. **Every extractor and EXPLORE script writes through `common.write_new` or a versioned screenshot path and refuses to overwrite: a re-run writes `<name>.v2.<ext>` beside the old file (rule 6); readers use the newest version.**
  - `form_xrefs.py`: finds each form's VMT and every reference to it, so "reached from" claims come from code (`form_xrefs.tsv`).
  - `extract_dump_strings.py` and `extract_strings.py`: every string literal of the whole-application Ghidra dump by function (`dump_string_literals.tsv`: 376 literals with a letter; `dump_string_literals.v2.tsv`: 485 literals in 106 functions, now including punctuation-only ones such as the single space and the 59-dash banner that the news check needs) and every `AnsiString` literal of the exe (`exe_strings.tsv`); `delphi_symbols.tsv` is a copy of the symbol table.
  - `hlp_dir.py`, `hlp_topics.py`, `extract_help.py`, `hlp_check.py`: a small WinHelp 3.x reader written for this task (no tool installed): directory B+tree, LZ77 topic blocks, the Win95 phrase table (`|PhrIndex`, `|PhrImage`). The phrase-code decoder is **recovered empirically**, not from a specification: the text is readable but loses a few characters in most paragraphs (`help_decode_check.txt`: 66 of 247 compressed records have the declared length). Outputs: `help_topics.tsv` (71 topics), `help_contents_entries.tsv` (the 89 `.cnt` entries; the `.cnt` itself is a game file and is not tracked). The WinHelp viewer of Wine shows the real text (`FI_b1_06_help_topics.png`).
  - `show_fn.py` prints a function of the dump by name or address; `extract_report_titles.py` snapshots the research reports' titles (`report_titles.tsv`) for the rules sweep.
- **Existing reports first.** Rows cite the research repository's `docs/reports/` (`R:`), this repository's `findings/` (`F:`), `coverage.md` (`C:`) and `tests/results.md`; nothing of them is redone.
- **EXPLORE batch 2 (rework, Wine, about 15 minutes, `explore_b2.py`).** The three Unit-map submenus are located by OCR, hovered and **proven open (expected item names read back, hash different from the parent menu) before the screenshot is kept**: the batch-1 Fleet and City submenu shots were byte-identical to the parent menu (same SHA-256) and no row cites them any more. It also shows the **Show hints** toggle (the speed-button hint present, absent after one click, back after a second) and runs the Shift and Ctrl map keys while another nation is viewed. 18 screenshots, `MANIFEST-batch2.txt`, release asset `FI_batch2_screenshots.tar.gz`.
- **EXPLORE batch 1 (Wine, about 40 minutes, own Xvfb `:700`, own copy of the game folder, fixture `BASE.SAV` = the run-0 start, seed 12345; scripts `explore_*.py`, text log `explore/b1.log`).** It settled what the sources left open: the Help menu, About and its CAncell button, Help topics, New player / New nation / Abdicate prompts, every menu, Balance sheet, Find a city, the nation status panel (own and foreign), Show mercenaries, a city's left and right click, Ctrl+Q against Ctrl+P. No End turn was clicked, no order was issued, no player save was touched. 43 screenshots, hashed in `SAVES.sha256`, are in the release `run-exp-feature-inventory` as `FI_batch1_screenshots.tar.gz` with the tracked `MANIFEST-batch1.txt`.
- **Evidence labels in the table.** `R:` research report and section; `F:` finding of this repository; `C:§n` a `coverage.md` row; `X:` a function of the dump (addresses in `delphi_symbols.tsv`); `form` a `TPF0` resource; `H:` a help topic; `SS:` a screenshot of batch 1 (bare file name). Saves are cited by bare file name (`T_*.SAV` are the order-test saves of `tests/results.md`).
- **Tags.** `[confirmed]`: seen in play, with a save, a screenshot listed in the tracked manifest, or a `coverage.md` row with status ✅ cited; **a report or a log is never confirmation**. The tag covers what the cited evidence shows; sentences marked `[code]`, `[help]`, `[report]` or `[form]` inside a row are attributed to that source and were not tested, and the Preconditions column comes from the cited code, help or report unless a save is named. `[derived]`: read from the code, a form, a help topic or a report, not seen in play here. `[candidate]`: reserved for Wine-only or inferred; none needed.
- **Need.** needed / useful / cosmetic is the author's reading of whether a player needs the feature to play a game end to end; it is a judgment, not evidence (see Inferences).

## Row-versus-source audit

`row_source_audit.tsv` (built by `build_row_audit.py` from `row_audit.cfg`) lists **161 source conditions over 37 rows**: for each, the source file and line, the exact quote, and the key the row text must carry. It covers every E row (E01-E29) and the [derived] rows whose tracked source states a gate (S02b, S02d, UA01, MM05, V01-V04). The checker verifies that each quote is on its line of a tracked file (`docs/rules-digest.md`, a finding) and that each key is present in the row; the conditions of E01 that only a research report states are marked `R:` (the report is not tracked, so the checker cannot read it). The other [derived] rows describe forms, functions or help topics and carry no thresholds. The round-2 review named five rows that had dropped gates (E12, E13, E14, E17, E26); each is restored and audited.

## Inferences

Nothing in the table rests on these.

- **Need column.** "needed" marks what the author judges a player must be able to do to finish a game: end the turn, move and attack, recruit and mobilize, tax, relations, supply, fortify, build fleets and embark (the help says a fleet is essential to cross seas), and the end-of-game screens. This is a judgment from the help text and the rules digest, not a measurement.
- **Cellular Automata.** A window reached only through a misspelled button of the About box is probably a development leftover or an easter egg; the code gives no purpose. The row is "cosmetic" on that reading.
- **Show hints.** The toggle is shown (hint present, absent after one click, present again after a second) and its handler is read (`TPremierForm_ToggleHints` flips the application's ShowHint, the item's check and one per-nation byte); what that per-nation byte is for is not known.
- **Two empty help topics.** The last two topic records have no title and no text in the decoder; they are probably images or jump targets, not pages a player reads.
- **A clone check.** A clone that wires every row marked `[confirmed]` or `[derived]` as "needed" and "useful" reaches every order of the original; whether it then plays "end to end" depends on rows outside this inventory (the tactical battle).

## What this does not establish

- **The clone.** It was not on this machine; the answer to "which features the clone's menu never listed" is given from the original side only.
- **The tactical battle** is excluded as asked (`TBattleMap`, `TBattleDelays`, their strings and help topics). `TBattleOver` is listed once (row D11).
- **Desktop Windows.** Every `[confirmed]` row was seen under Wine 9.0 with the patched `fast rollingsave seed` build, not on the original install.
- **`[derived]` rows were not run.** In particular: File > Save (the driver uses Save As), the Area-map armies / fleets / all views, the foreign-army panel, the fleet panel, the human-against-human peace box, the AI alliance line, the capital-move and fleet-launch news lines, the quarterly tick, supply use by season, victory at 334 cities, the 250 BC end, conquest, and both deposition screens. `coverage.md` §4 lists most of them as not yet seen.
- **Help text.** The decoder is empirical and loses characters (`help_decode_check.txt`); it was used for captions, numbers and nouns, and the Wine WinHelp viewer was shown for the first topic only.
- **The 71 decoded topics versus the 89 `.cnt` entries.** The `.cnt` repeats topics (a command is listed under Menu Commands and again under Area map or Unit map) and has 13 headings with no topic; the mapping is by title and is in `coverage_entries.tsv`.
- **Whether each help claim holds in play** (for example the 15 percent supply rule for mercenaries, the 1,000-talent cap on money moved while supplying): cited from help and code, not measured here.
- **The sweep rows E12 to E29 were not run.** They are `[derived]` from reports and code; the completeness claim is bounded by the three sources swept (rules digest, `coverage.md` §4, the report titles) and by whatever those omit.
- **Win and defeat rows** come from `THumanFalls_InitializeForm` and the reports; none was reached in play.
