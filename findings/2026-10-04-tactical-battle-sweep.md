# Tactical battle sweep, Stage 1 (battles plan B1 to B5 and B8): the battle block decoded, crafted saves, 315 battles of 25 pairings x sizes x 3 seeds, the icon thresholds

**Status:** draft finding from `ic2-conquest` (the deliverable of the research request, §0.2 of `docs/proposals/battles.md`), awaiting promotion; **Wine-only: every result is a candidate until the desktop original confirms it.** Branch `experiment/battle-sweep-b5` (PR "battles: B5 sweep and B8 screenshots (Stage 1)"). Data (text, tracked) in `runs/experiments/data/run-exp-battle-sweep/`; saves and screenshots in three releases (the 1000-asset limit of one release was hit: see that folder's README, section "Where the saves and screenshots are"): `run-exp-battle-sweep`, `run-exp-battle-sweep-b5`, `run-exp-battle-sweep-2`; SHA-256 of every file in `SAVES.sha256`, archive members in `release-manifest-*.json` / `MANIFEST-b8.txt`. Tags: **[O]** observed, **[D]** derived from the block, **[R-code]** taken from the research repo's decompiled battle module (`docs/reports/2026-10-04-decompiled-tactical-battle-rules.md`, read-only input; each use is checked against the data and says so), **[?]** unknown. Everything is **lab** (battle-lab build, seed baked in) and **L1** (strategic edit of `FLD-RG_0743_rome_army0_at_86_28.SAV` before the attack, placement and initiative the game's own) unless labelled L2 (block edit of a mid-battle save, B3).

**What 3 seeds per cell mean.** Each cell of the sweep has 3 battles (seeds 1, 2, 3). They describe **how a battle unfolds, not the odds**: a win rate needs the 30 seeds of battles plan B10. "Rome wins 3 of 3" below is a description of three battles. Where a column says "winner" it is the side whose army survived in the post-battle save.

**Answer (B5, B8).**
- **315 battles done, 0 error records** (225 grid battles: 25 ordered pairings, attacker Rome = type A, defender Gaul = type B, one unit of `half` / `one` standard battalion or `three` units, seeds 1-3; plus 90 size-matrix battles: HI-HI, HI>LI, LI>HI, LC>Ar, Ar>HC at the 6 off-diagonal size combinations), every one both sides on Computer general, quality 6, morale 65, the edited armies **read back from game memory before the attack** (a mismatch raises and is an error record) (`trials.jsonl`, `sweep-table-20261004-162347.csv`, `b5-pairings-20261004-193033.csv`, matrices `b5-matrix-20261004-193232.md`). **52.0 s per battle** (49.0 to 61.1 s, process start to post-battle save), 15.2 half-rounds on average (8 to 35); 279 battles needed no End turn click, 36 needed one (each proven by the half-round counter; none unproven).
- **Rome (the attacker) won 133 and Gaul 182.** Within a type pairing the result is the same at all three sizes in most cells: **HI as attacker beat LI, Ar, LC and HC in 36 of 36 grid battles and HI v HI went to Gaul in 8 of 9; archers as attacker won 0 of 45; every same-type pairing went to the defender except HI-HI three v three (1 of 3)**. The matrix is in `b5-matrix-20261004-193232.md`. In the size matrix Rome (attacker) won: three v half 15 of 15, three v one 12 of 15, one v half 9 of 15, half v one 6 of 15, one v three 3 of 15, half v three 0 of 15 (`b5-matrix-20261004-193232.md`): the larger side tends to win, but one v half is not decided by size alone.
- **The Offer of peace appeared in 76 of 315 rows, all after a Gaul win**; always captured and declined (No), never Yes.
- **B8: the icon of a unit changes at exactly `std div 3` and `2 x (std div 3)` troops, on both sides, to the exact troop:** LI 5,000 / 10,000, HI 2,000 / 4,000, Ar 1,166 / 2,332, LC 2,333 / 4,666, HC 833 / 1,666 (the first troop count of size 1 and size 2; one troop below, the lower size) (`b8-thresholds-20261004-192949.json`, `b8-icon-index-20261004-192949.csv`). This matches the decompiled rule `size = min(2, troops div (std div 3))` [R-code] and **corrects B2's hypothesis `3 x troops < std`, which is off by 1 or 2 troops for Ar, LC and HC** (B2 had only the gaps between observed troop counts). One icon image per (type, side, size): 30 combinations, 30 distinct icons.

**Answer (B1 to B4).**
- **The battle block is decoded** (B2): 9 header bytes, 40 slots of 44 bytes (position x, y, origin label, type, troops, quality, battle-local morale, a movement-points field, an ammunition field, a target slot, the name) and a 14 × 12 grid of unit **sprites** (`state/battle_block.py`). The block is in game memory at **0x4A0344** (slots and grid, contiguous) with its header fields at 0x4A0B74..0x4A0B7D, so `Game.battle_state()` reads a live battle without saves: it was equal to File > Save As's block 12 in all 3 phases tried (`b2-verify-20261004-093834.json`). The **slot positions ↔ screen mapping** holds on **all 168 cells in all three phases** (each cell classified occupied or empty from the screenshot against the cells the slots occupy; the grid words are checked against the slots separately, below) (`B2_placement.SAV`, `B2_after_end_turn_1.SAV`, `B2_after_end_turn_2.SAV`, screenshots `b2_*_window.png`). **The grid words against the drawn icons** (`b2-icons-20261004-111640.json`, offline from those screenshots and saves): over the 42 occupied cells of the three phases (14 per phase, 11 distinct grid words) every grid word has exactly one icon image (inner 26 × 26 pixels of the tile) and different words have different icons, so the grid word identifies the drawn sprite. **Which `b2_probe.py` run the B2 claims rest on:** the completed run `b2-verify-20261004-093834.json` (End turn clicks 1 and 2 each proven by the half-round counter, no retry). Two earlier runs aborted: `-093449` after its End turn click 2 gave no sign (single click, no retry in that version) and `-093600` retried after a no-sign timeout and the retry also gave no sign (`b2-verify-20261004-093600.log`); neither wrote a phase after that second click, so no B2 claim rests on them. The probe's retry has been removed.
- **The research request's "slot word +2" is the unit's origin label** (slot word index 2 = byte +4 of a 44-byte slot, `DAT_004a0348` = 0x4A0344 + 4): 0 for a regular unit, 11 for the Gallic mercenary, equal to the strategic unit's label for every unit. It is **not** a link to the army's unit index (hypothesis rejected; the slot order itself is the link, below). The header word at bytes 2-3 is the **defender's strategic army index** (10).
- **Crafted mid-battle saves are accepted and play from the edited state** (B3, L2): a no-op block edit is byte-identical and resumes to a byte-identical series (`CRAFT_c0_control.SAV` v `CRAFT_c1_noop.SAV`); a troops edit, a type edit and an adjacency edit change the battle as expected; the game **refills the movement-points field on resume** and does **not** repair an inconsistent grid.
- **B4: HI v HI, one unit each, seeds 1 to 3, both sides on Computer general, each seed run four times: all four runs of a seed are byte-identical** (all `BATTLEnn.SAV` of the series and the post-battle save, `compare-hi-hi-one_s{1,2,3}_r1-*.json`), **53.4 s per battle** on average (48.4 to 58.5 s, 12 trials, process start to post-battle save). Rome (the attacker) lost all three; Gaul kept 2,958 / 2,831 / 4,196 of 6,000 in 19 / 18 / 15 half-rounds.

## Method

- **Start state:** `FLD-RG_0743_rome_army0_at_86_28.SAV` (PR #35; the research start save `1_rome_270_winter_11.sav` with Rome's army 0 walked to (86,28), next to Gaul's army 10 at (85,28)), built twice byte-identical (`39edecd1…`). Natural (nothing edited) for B2; for B4 the L1 edits of `runs/experiments/battles/stage.py`: army 0 and army 10 each set to one HI unit of 6,000, quality 6, army morale 65 (`hi-hi-one_start.SAV`). Army positions are never edited.
- **Builds:** lab exes `Imperial Conquest 2 lab s1/s2/s3.exe` (`setup/build_lab_exes.sh`, SHA-256 in `EXES-sha256.txt`). One process per battle. Wine 9.0, Xvfb 1280 × 1024.
- **Decoder:** reverse-engineered from B0's `gate2_a` and `gate2_s2a` series (33 files) and the trials' series, then checked on **241 decoded files** of the series (`b2-analysis-20261004-110552.json`: B0's two series, 33 files, and the 12 trials, 208 files) and, in the complete pass, on **763 `BATTLE<nn>` saves** (the same file, `all_battle_saves`), against game memory and against screenshots (`b2_probe.py`).
- **Crafted saves:** `stage.block_edit` on `hi-hi-one_s1_r1_BATTLE02.SAV` / `BATTLE03.SAV` (and B0's `gate2_a_BATTLE04.SAV`), resumed by File > Open on the lab seed-1 exe (`b3_crafted.py`).
- **Runner:** `runs/experiments/battles/trials.py` (PR #35); every End turn click goes through `Game.end_turn_proven` (flag, title, BATTLEnn count or the half-round counter must move within 8 s). The half-round log: `halflog.py`.

- **Sweep (B5):** `runs/experiments/battles/trials.py run <cell> --seeds 1-3` for each cell (`sweep_run.sh`, one batch per attacker type and size, then per size-matrix pairing; trials append to `trials.jsonl`, nothing is retried, an error would be a record), the L1 edits `stage.uniform` (one type per army; 3 units = 3 x the standard battalion), tabulation by `b5_analyze.py` (new files each run; the finding cites `-193033`). Each batch was committed and pushed; its saves uploaded (see the README for the releases).
- **Icons (B8):** `b8_ladder.py` (L1 ladder, 15 battles in v2), `b8_analyze.py`, `b8_layout.py` (hover tooltips, one click on a unit, the surrender box answered No, one proven End turn, Computer general to the end).

- **Slots and strategic armies.** Slots 0 to 19 are the attacker's army, 20 to 39 the defender's. In the live battle the AI side is **re-sorted at battle start** (the army's pre-battle order is not the slot order), while in a save written inside a battle army 10's records already follow the slots (`gate2_a_BATTLE05.SAV`). **`stage.block_edit` therefore never maps a slot to a unit by index: it matches the slot's (name, type) to exactly one unit of the army and refuses when that is ambiguous.** No B3 crafted save edited a defender slot (data README), so their results are not affected. The AI side is re-sorted at battle start (Gaul's HI first, then LI: army 10's records `7th Foot, 4th Guards, 8th Foot, 9th Foot, 5th Guards` are in slots as `4th Guards, 5th Guards, 8th Foot, 7th Foot, 9th Foot`, `b2-verify-20261004-093834.json` `side1`). The army records keep the **start** troops through the battle; the slots hold the live ones.
- **Memory.** `Game.battle_state()` = slots and grid at 0x4A0344 (2,096 bytes) plus header words at 0x4A0B74 (attacker army), 0x4A0B76 (defender army), 0x4A0B78 (`x2`), 0x4A0B7A (half-round counter) and the byte 0x4A0B7D (`y1`); the battle flag is 0x4A0B7C. Equal to the Save As block in 3 of 3 phases. **Unverified:** the attacker-army and `x2` addresses (both read 0 in the comparisons) and, after a resume, `x2` reads 0 where the file has 1 (`b3-crafted-20261004-095138.jsonl`, `gate2_a_BATTLE04.SAV`): either the resume resets it or the address is wrong.
- **Resume bumps the counter and refills moves.** After File > Open of a block save the counter reads +1 (2 → 3, 3 → 4) or +2 (4 → 6) and every unit's `state` is its type's movement allowance.
- **Diff and inferred actions.** The two natural 14-unit battles (B0's `gate2_a`, `gate2_s2a`): 125 loss rows, **14 (11 %) unambiguous** (exactly one adjacent enemy or one shooter, `BB.diff`); the rest are `unknown`. In the 1-v-1 battles of B4 **every** loss row is unambiguous (272 of 272 in the 12 trials; 26 of 26 for seed 1; `b2-analysis-20261004-110552.json` `per_group`). (The aggregate over both groups is 286 of 397, 72 %: it mixes the two kinds of battle.) Position, troops, quality and morale are exact; kind and actor are [D].

The 25 x 3 winner matrix with the half-round ranges is `b5-matrix-20261004-193232.md`. Saves are cited by the bare names in the rows (`<cell>_s<seed>_r1_BATTLE<nn>.SAV`, `<cell>_s<seed>_r1_post.SAV`, `<cell>_start.SAV`), found in the three releases above.

  Rome wins 88 of the 225 grid battles (29 half, 28 one, 31 three) and 45 of the 90 matrix battles. In the only two cells that are not the same at every size and seed, li>hc (half GAA, one GAG, three AAA) and hi>hi three v three (AGG), the seeds disagree, which is a reminder that 3 seeds are not odds. **This table includes the attacker/defender asymmetry of the geometry** (Rome approaches from the east, Gaul is placed first on row y = 9): every same-type pairing goes to Gaul except one cell, so "defender wins" mixes type strength with side.
- **Placement [O], from the first two files of every battle** (`b5-placement-20261004-193033.csv`, `halfrounds-*.jsonl`): in the first file (half-round 1) Gaul's units stand on row y = 9 in every battle, Rome's units are **parked on row y = 0** from x = 0; in the second file Rome's units have been placed on **row y = 2** (315 of 315). Gaul's units stand side by side (one cell apart, x = a, a+1, a+2 for three units), wrapping to the next row for more than 3 units (B8 found the block is 3 wide: ladder v1); the starting x of Gaul's block depends on the **seed and Gaul's army**, not on Rome's: **for the same seed and the same defender army the Gaul placement was identical in every cell** (45 groups: 24 groups of one cell, 9 of two, 12 of five attacker types; `b5-summary-20261004-193033.json` `seed_pairing_gaul_placement_by_defender_army_and_seed`). Rome's placement x varies by type: HI, HC and Ar mostly start at x = 4 or 7 or 10, LI at x = 1 or 7, LC at x = 1 or 10 (counts in the same file, `placement_attacker_second_file_x_by_type`): the AI general places by type, which this sweep describes but does not derive. [R-code] says each side's units are placed type by type in slot order filling columns then rows; the data is consistent with it.
- **Initiative [D]** (`acting_sides_D` of the diffs into files 2 to 6, 315 of 315 battles: Rome, Rome, Gaul, Rome, Gaul): the first file is the placement, Rome acts in the second (its placement) and third (its first move), then the sides alternate Gaul, Rome, Gaul. Header `x2` equals the acting side in all 3,220 diffs with exactly one acting side, so **`x2` is the side to move** [R-code `0x4A0B78`, confirmed by the data]. [R-code] says the AI moves types in the order HI, HC, LC, LI, Ar, units of a type in slot order: the **per-unit order inside a half-round cannot be read from end-of-half-round snapshots**, so this is neither confirmed nor contradicted here.
- **Target choice [O, as observations of the AI general, not rules]** (`b5-targets-20261004-193033.csv`): in the **664** half-rounds in which a unit's target word changed to an enemy slot while at least two enemies stood (3 v 3 and mixed-size battles), **the chosen enemy was among the nearest (Chebyshev distance) in 664 of 664**, **uniquely** the nearest in 147 (in the others another enemy was at the same distance), and the weakest by troops (ties included) in 507. 1 v 1 battles have one enemy and no choice. Caution: with units standing in a block against a block, nearest is often forced by the geometry, and the units that pick a target are the ones in contact.
- **Attribution of losses [D, R-code words 8 and 9]** (`BB.attribute`, `b5-summary-20261004-193033.json`): the `target` field is the melee target (word 9) and `ammo` the shots left (word 8) [R-code]; a BATTLEnn snapshot is taken before the side's melee, so the targets in a file are those of the melee that follows. With them **29 of the 125 loss rows of B0's natural 14-unit battles are fixed (old diff-only rule: 14, 11 %)**, **1,569 of 1,931 in the 1 v 1 sweep battles (old 1,481)**, **1,166 of 2,805 in the 3 v 3 battles (old 627)** and 624 of 987 in the size matrix (old 574). Sweep kinds: melee 4,263, shooting 371, **mixed 1,089** (a melee link and a shooter in the same diff: not fixed), unknown 0. **What words 8 and 9 cannot fix:** the target of a shot (the game stores no shooting target: the shooter is known from the drop of its `ammo`, which loss row it hit is not when two enemies lost troops), and a melee row where the victim is in several melee links.
- **End conditions [D].** The strategic result only says the loser's army is destroyed (182 attacker armies, 133 defender armies; never both, never neither). The last saved half-round is the state before the final half-round: in 58 battles the loser has no unit left in it, in 238 it has units, **none of them eligible for a rout** (troops at least `std div 25` and morale above 39 [R-code]), so they were killed by troop loss in the final half-round, and in 19 at least one unit was rout-eligible (kill or rout, not distinguished). **Surrender is never seen** (Computer general does not surrender). No battle ran near the 99-half-round limit (max 35). [R-code] says nothing else ends a battle.

## Inferences

- **The slot order is the link between a battle and its strategic army**, not a per-slot index word; the "word +2" of the research request is the origin label.
- **The random stream is consumed by the exchanges, not by movement:** five different geometries of the same HI v HI battle after a resume (d0 to d8: adjacent, far away, same cell, stale grid) end in the same post-battle save (Gaul 3,285 and Rome destroyed), only the number of half-rounds changes. So, for a fixed seed, timing and placement alter *when* the exchanges happen, not their results, at least for this pairing. [D]
- **The grid is a drawing aid** (sprites; no terrain in these battles); the game plays from the slots and does not repair the grid.
- **The icon ladder needs L1, not L2** (B8): a block edit of troops does not make the game choose a new sprite; the game's own sprite rule is only visible when it builds the grid itself (at placement from the strategic armies). L2 crafted saves remain useful for placing units after the placement phase.
- **Cells of the sweep are not paired samples** beyond the placement draws (B5, pairing seeds).

## What this does not establish

- **Win rates or odds:** 3 seeds per cell describe how battles unfold; B10 (30 seeds) gives odds. The sweep is **one geometry** (Rome from the east, Gaul placed first on row y = 9, no terrain), armies of **one type each** at quality 6 and morale 65, so mixed armies (B6), the attacker side swapped (B6), terrain (B9) and human play (B7) are not covered.
- **The order of units inside a half-round, the exchanges themselves and which of two losing units a shot hit:** snapshots are half-round states; the exchange-level hook (B11) is not built.
- **Whether a battle ever ends by rout or surrender** (no unit was seen routed in an image; the end-state classification in B5 is inferred from rout eligibility [R-code]).
- That the normal (non-lab) build behaves like the lab build in these pairings (one sweep cell on the normal build was not re-run).
- The meaning of `y1`; `x2` is the side to move in every diff with one acting side (B5), its memory address is unverified; the memory addresses of the attacker-army and `x2` header words; why Gaul's LI `state` reads 1 not 4; what `ammo` falls by for each unit type (only archers' 4 per volley is clear).
- That `state` is exactly "movement points left" (it fits the type's allowance and the moves seen), (the size-class thresholds are now exact, B8).
- Whether the normal build resumes a block save as the lab build does; whether edits during placement could ever stick (L2 positions at placement are overwritten).
- **Wine-only.**
