## 1. The stale DBA8 word across 24 AI turns: −1 reaches only the next EBE8 walk

`walk_hook` (probes inside DBA8's frame) ran from 8 starts, 3 End turns each: Ptolemaic t01, t13, t21; Macedonia t19, t26,
t33; Numidia t09 and t37. Run `hooks-walk-multi-v1`; **24 of 24 hooked autosaves are byte-identical** to the plain build.
The summary is in `walk-stale-survey-v1.txt`.

| | Count |
|---|---|
| walks (A records) | 393: 316 direct, 77 through `FUN_0044db38` |
| walks whose DB38 found no intermediate point (word −1 at the B/C tests) | **2**, both Ptolemaic army 13's E84C walk (`e913`) toward (192,77), Numidia hook turns 1 and 3 |
| walks with −1 **at entry** | **1**: the EBE8 walk (`ecd6`) of the same army, right after, turn 1 |
| the walk after a failed DB38 that did **not** inherit −1 | turn 3: the same army's F31C walk at 0x44F4C7 (return `0x44f4cc`), entry word 49; turn 1, after EBE8: the next seat's first walk (Galatia, `f47c`), 49 |
| every other entry word | 49, 71 or 0, as in #97 |

E84C and EBE8 are both called from F31C and have the same prologue (`5356575551`), so their DBA8 frames coincide. F31C's own
walks (0x44F409, 0x44F43F, 0x44F47C, 0x44F4A3, 0x44F4C7) sit one level higher and read other values, as do other seats. This
supports, in 24 more turns, the narrow rule research specified in PR #188:
- a walk whose DB38 fails skips its own swing and recursion;
- the same army's following EBE8 direct walk skips them too;
- otherwise T239 applies.

Not measured: EBE8 after a failed E84C walk taking the DB38 path itself (DB38 then writes its own word anyway).

## 2. Gap C was a harness artefact

Gap C, open since v0.9.0, is the AI fleet next to its port: it stays in the original and sails in the remake (Alexandria in
the Rome and Carthage games, Salamis in the Macedonia game). Replayed with per-turn seeds (§3):

| Case | Original | Remake, fixed import seed (v0.9.1, v0.9.2) | Remake, `--turn-seed 12345` |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rome t3, t17, Ptolemaic fleet 1 | (189,93), 6 moves | (188,92), 0 moves | **(189,93), 6 moves** |
| Macedonia t23, Ptolemaic fleet 1 | (202,74), 4 moves | (208,82), 0 moves | **(202,74), 4 moves** |

With independent draws the remake does what the original does. The fixed seed and `TurnIndex` of every imported save
(`2026-11-03-remake-v092-recheck.md` §4) repeated one draw that sent the fleet out. Which draw (most likely the seat order
against the mid-round refill) was not traced. **No port rule is wrong.** The `patches/e1fc_hook.py` built for this (two
probes inside `FUN_0044e1fc`: the replaced target, and `FUN_0044d174`'s result that decides whether `FUN_0044e094` walks) was
not needed and has not been run.

## 4. Numidia: the logged RandSeed reproduces the campaign's rounds exactly

The 26 position rows of the turn-seeded Numidia replay come from 13 turns. For each turn, the start is the campaign's
checkpoint before End turn, or the previous turn's last checkpoint when the human gave no orders (`decidestarts-num/`,
`SOURCES.json`). The campaign's logged RandSeed before that End turn (`num-start-randseeds-v2.json`) is written to 0x45E030
after loading: `decide_hook_run.py` now honours `IC2_START_RANDSEEDS`.

**Result** (`hooks-decide-num-v1`, 13 of 13 inert): in **all 13 turns the hooked original's army and fleet tables equal the
campaign's**. So the logged seed replays the campaign's round exactly, and the paint routines' reseeding does not get in the
way. Every row's original tile is reproduced (`positions-numidia-seeded-v1.txt`). The remaining differences are therefore the
remake's own play under its own draws.

**Grouped by the round's events** (`numidia-rows-by-round-v1.txt`):

`-t11pre` were the **end** of their turns (those turns have a single checkpoint). So `probes/1723c673-num-t09pre` ran on turn

## 5. A Greece long run on v0.9.2

Greece, rulebot-1, 40 turns, seed 12345, RandSeed logged (`run-exp-longrun-v092/greece-rulebot1-40t`). 40 turns, no game
over.
- **Classify:** 138 relation nation-turns, 23 position, 3 count, 2 owner, 16 draw-only turns.
- **Stays:** 4 rows with the fixed seed, **2 with per-turn seeds**:
  - **Carthage t6** (capital army): Carthage declares war on Rome in the remake's round, a roll.
  - **Seleucid t21:** army 2 at (221,60), next to Antioch, ends at (221,64) in the remake.
    - **Original** (decide hook with the campaign's seed, `hooks-decide-gre-v1`, inert, reproduces the campaign's armies):
      9 moves at the seat, the resupply branch, home city 259 Antioch (adjacent capital).
    - **Remake** (`probes/1723c673-gre-t21pre-seed12366`): "move to the resupply city (221, 65)", Aradus.
    - **The new `resupplyprobe`** (`difftest/remake_adapter/ResupplyProbe.cs`) on the start state: the remake's
      `ScoreResupplyCity` and `Decide` also choose **Antioch** (score 19 = −1 + 20 for the capital). So the remake's scorer
      agrees; its state at Seleucid's seat (second to last in that round, after its resupply pass of 127 tons) makes it choose
      Aradus. Which state change does that is **not identified**. The walk hook on this start (`hooks-walk-gre-v1`) is in the
      data.
