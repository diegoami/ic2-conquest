# Italy before Roman hegemony: bounded scenario-source proposal

**Status:** proposal for research39/build900, future0.7 preparation. No final
date/roster, map/content implementation, balance approval or release-gate
change. Main session researched public sources; GLM5.3Flash review selected.
Current0.6 battle/export work remains separate. Sources checked2026-10-21.

## What the proposed campaign means

Interpret the working title as **before Roman hegemony**, not before Rome
existed: Rome can be one regional contestant alongside Etruscan cities, Veneti,
Samnite and other Italic communities and Greek poleis. If the owner literally
means a pre-foundation setting, the date and requested faction vocabulary need
a different archaeological brief; do not silently move all these fourth-century
representations into the early Iron Age.

Evidence, inference and design are labeled below. Ancient literary accounts
are retrospective, not contemporary censuses or GIS boundaries. Archaeological
culture, language and a playable political controller are different things.

trait or an objective ethnographic description.

## Representation rules supported by the sources

- **Etruscan cities:** cultural connections do not establish a permanent
  unified empire. The Met locates the central homeland between Arno/Tiber and
  describes wider expansion; Penn's archaeological discussion distinguishes
  Etruscan states and evidence from biased literary traditions [S1,S9]. Any
  single controller is an explicit gameplay coalition, not an attested central
  government with fixed modern-style borders.
- **Veneti:** the national museum identifies northeastern material culture and
  Este's importance; its settlement display puts major urban development in
  the sixth–fourth centuries [S2]. Polybius2.17 distinguishes Veneti from Celtic
  neighbors [S10]. These do not supply one uniform government or immutable
  territory for every proposed date.
- **Oscans/Samnites:** avoid two overlapping nation-states called “Oscans” and
  “Samnites.” Use an Oscan language/cultural tag and named regional political
  abstractions such as Samnite communities and Campanian communities. The
  Molise museum distinguishes Pentrian public cult/administration and earlier
  occupation from much later monumental buildings [S6]; do not give400 BCE
  armies a second-century theatre-temple as an existing state capital.
- **Greek colonies:** Taras, Croton, Locri/Rhegion and Syracuse must not be
  automatically one “Greeks” nation. The Tarentine object record establishes
  a southeastern Greek center and exchange context, not troop/economy totals
  [S7]. Leontinoi's official site history records changing dependence and
  autonomy, while Diodorus's405 settlement distinguishes tributaries,
  autonomous communities and Syracuse [S4,S11]. Simplifying that structure
  into the game's relations requires a labeled design decision.

exceptions; no row grants ownership of every city in its modern region.

This uses all 16 slots. Ligurian, Sardinian and other omitted communities cannot
be silently assigned to neighboring states, erased, or tagged “barbarian.” The
owner must choose between different coalitions, a narrower playable footprint,
or a supported neutral/community representation. That representation decision
is a real remaining prerequisite, not permission to exceed16 or ship an
ethnographically empty map. A343 variant must redo this budget, not merely
change the calendar label.

## Map extent, scale and a substantial settlement network

**DESIGNED extent:** approximately6.5–19degreesE,35.5–46.8degreesN for a
peninsula-centered campaign including Sicily, adjacent Tyrrhenian/Adriatic seas
and a narrow northern-African Punic anchor. Sardinia/Corsica inside the rectangle
need an explicit playable/excluded/neutral policy as above. No modern coastline
polygon determines ancient political ownership.

Use a regional projection centered near42N/12E and physically comparable cell
dimensions, rather than squeezing Italy into the original game's wide320x140
ratio. A320x384 grid is a candidate budget (about0.83 width/height), subject
to actual projection, UI/pathfinding capacity and performance checks. This is
not a claim that a particular engine limit or content contract already permits
that size. Provide Alps/Po plains, Apennine passes, coastal routes and the
Strait of Messina as real campaign choices; cities must connect via navigable
routes and supplies, not sit on a tiny demonstration map.

Target roughly48–64 dated settlements for the first complete campaign. The
following48-name pool is a **verification backlog**, not approved400 BCE city
ownership/population or tile coordinates:

|Region/purpose|Six candidate names to verify at selected date|
|---|---|
|Northern routes|Ateste,Patavium,Opitergium,Adria,Felsina,Spina|
|Northern/inner Etruria|Arretium,Cortona,Clusium,Volaterrae,Populonia,Perusia|
|Southern Etruria/Tiber crossings|Tarquinii,Vulci,Caere,Veii,Falerii,Volsinii|
|Latium|Roma,Tusculum,Aricia,Praeneste,Tibur,Lanuvium|
|Central/eastern/interior|Iguvium,Tuder,Asculum,Ancona,Bovianum,Saepinum|
|Campanian coast/interior|Capua,Cumae,Neapolis,Nola,Nuceria,Poseidonia|
|Southern Greek sea routes|Taras,Metapontion,Thurii,Croton,Locri,Rhegion|
|Sicilian routes|Syracuse,Leontinoi,Messana,Akragas,Gela,Motya|

Some are intentionally date-sensitive: Veii cannot retain unchanged independent
ownership after its traditional396 capture [S3]; Leontinoi's status cannot be
inferred from a Greek name [S11]; later urban forms/Latin aliases at interior
sites do not prove equivalent cities existed in400. Motya's chronology must be
checked before any later-date variant. If a site fails dating, replace it with
a documented contemporary settlement; do not invent an early foundation.
The pool also needs further Samnite/Messapian/Sicilian-indigenous anchors and
an African/Punic city to give every controller a viable, sourced start.

Each accepted city needs a small source row: persistent place/site ID; ancient
and modern aliases; dated occupation and confidence; longitude/latitude plus
precision; archaeological/museum citation; proposed controller with separate
evidence/design tag; tile projection recipe and source attribution. Settlement
location is not a territory boundary or a garrison-size estimate. One checked
Taras cross-reference is the university Tabula Peutingeriana record [S12]; its
later map context is a geographic crosswalk, not evidence of400 BCE ownership.

## Geographic provenance and reuse

- Natural Earth offers public-domain vector/raster geography [S13]. Use pinned
  versions for coastline/base terrain references, preserve original metadata
  and a transformation recipe. Modern coastlines/rivers are starting geometry,
  not ancient shorelines: ancient deltas, lagoons and river courses need site
  evidence. Do not derive roads or historical borders from modern layers.
- Pleiades policies state CC BY3.0 [S14]. Retain per-record citations/contributors,
  retrieval/version and uncertainty; verify applicable downloaded-record terms.
  Direct place pages were bot-challenged during this pass, so no uninspected
  Pleiades coordinate dump or precise city placement is claimed. Get authorized
  public exports or direct site records before authoring, without bypassing
  access restrictions. Gazetteer IDs are not sufficient evidence of occupation
  on the chosen date.
- Museum/site prose and primary-text translations are cited here; their maps,
  photos, museum logos and art are not copied. Do not treat a site's public
  web page as a reusable-art license. No proprietary original-game map bytes
  or unattributed atlas drawing enters either repository.

## Relationships, forces/economy and victory are design until proved otherwise

**DESIGNED first balance pass:** ordinary diplomacy and recruitment for every
playable faction; local rivalries rather than universal permanent wars; maritime
advantages for major ports; mountain defense/route constraints for interior
communities; several viable starts with no guaranteed Roman victory. Relations
derived from a specific event, such as the405 Sicilian treaty or343 conflict,
must be tagged with date/source and adapted only through a reviewed design.

Use relative capacity bands before tuning numbers: port trade/naval logistics,
fertile plains food/tax potential, inland supply/movement constraints. None of
these supports an exact treasury, population, regiment count or unit stat in
this draft. Do not back-project Polybius's much later levy figures into400 BCE.

Candidate victory options, explicitly DESIGN: hold a coherent regional base
plus strategic routes/ports for a sustained period; survive a stronger rival
while preserving independent centers; optional broader hegemony target.
Campaign length, city-share threshold and asymmetric objectives require
playtesting/owner choice, not fabricated historical mandates.

“Barbarian” requires its own decisions: ordinary playable status versus special
controller; diplomacy/trade rights; whether armies spawn and from what finite
resources; victory eligibility; naming/UI and relation to settlements. No
ancient ethnic label automatically disables diplomacy, grants endless armies,
changes AI aggression or excludes victory. The ordinary16-slot budget above
contains no approved special barbarian mechanic.

## Minimum next research, later detail and owner choices

Before a substantial playable authoring contract: choose date and map/neutral
scope; approve the16-slot abstractions; audit48–64contemporary settlements and
their ownership uncertainty; create licensed geographic manifest and projected
route graph; source date-specific relationships; agree designed forces/economy
and objectives; validate human/AI routes, supply and multiple viable starts.
Explicitly resolve omitted peoples/areas rather than quietly filling them in.

Later detail: fine tribal subdivisions, specialist equipment, exact leader
portraits, minor shrines, linguistic display variants and granular event chains.
Do not block the core campaign on unattainable exact ancient troop counts.

Open owner choices are the preferred date, degree of coalition abstraction,
Sicily/Punic/island scope and ordinary-versus-special barbarian behavior. This
report supplies options and a researched acquisition plan; it is not a completed
city database, final map or implementation-ready ownership contract.
