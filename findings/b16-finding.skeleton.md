## Inferences

- **[D] Why the %%npairs_loss%% D-LOSS pairs look alike.** The box reseeds `RandSeed := winner + loser` when it opens (`%%reseed_site%%`, hooked records `loss_s10_hook-capture_r1` etc.), so everything after the box starts from the same stream whatever the seed; the seeds differ only in the battle's survivors (the post-answer saves of seeds %%sd_seeds%% differ in %%sd_post%% bytes, the turn-%%nturns%% autosaves in %%sd_turn%%). The %%npairs_loss%% pairs are therefore close replicates of one post-battle situation, not independent samples of the strategic aftermath; they are independent samples of the open/closed draw and of the identity proof.
- **[D] Yes writes the relation and the news and nothing else.** The full before/after diff of the %%fd_yes_runs%% Yes runs shows only the %%fd_yes_rel%% relation words and the news ring changing; the Yes and No post-answer saves differ in no other non-news field either. The report's "honourable peace with no reparations" matches: no money moved.
- **[D] The %%rel_yes_t1%% on the next turn** fits the quarterly thaw of a negative relation that the report gives %%lit:thaw%% acting on %%rel_yes_ans%% (a step of %%rel_step%%); it stays %%rel_yes_all%% over the next End turns, consistent with no further quarter boundary in them.
- **[D] After No Gaul keeps attacking Rome in the D-LOSS cell** because Rome's armies are weak there; that Gaul takes a city a turn is a property of this staged position (Rome's army %%rome_army%% gone, Gaul's army %%gaul_army%% adjacent), not a rate.
- **[R-code] and [O].** Measured: the draw is `Random(%%draw_range%%)` (range %%draw_range%% logged in all %%hook_n%% hooked runs), the box opens exactly when it is < %%draw_open%% (checked on every hooked run: %%hook_rule%%), it is drawn only when %%lit:t3%% pass, and the tests' thresholds are strict (`unity > %%g_unity_c1%%`, `cities > %%g_city_closed%%`, `armies(W) < armies(L)` at its extremes).

## What this does not establish

- **Wine-only.** The desktop original was not run.
- **Computer general on both sides**, no human-clicked battle. Whether a battle the human plays differently reaches the same box is not tested.
- **One geometry.** Rome v Gaul at (%%army0_x%%, %%army0_y%%) / (%%army10_x%%, %%army10_y%%), Rome's army %%rome_army%% or Gaul's army %%gaul_army%% reduced to one %%cell_troops%% %%cell_type%% battalion (L1 synthetic). The %%loss_pct%% % open rate is that of the draw at this geometry and does not depend on anything else here; it is not a property of every battle. The D-WIN opening needs the second-Gaul-army edit (an existing army re-owned, positions and map untouched; the unit map's marker for army %%ext_army%% was not looked at), and its %%ext_open%% of %%ext_n%% is a small sample.
- **The normal build for pairs, the lab hook build for the draw.** The hooked seeds are lab seeds; the pairing "this seed's draw" is not carried over to the normal build's seeds.
- **%%nturns%% End turns only**, and a position in which Rome's relations with its allies were not part of the question: the ally loop of the treaty (`%%lit:ally_cond%%`, %%lit:ally8%%) was not exercised (no case has Rome or Gaul with an ally at war with the other).
- **Not tested:** `armies(W)` equal to `armies(L)`; unity or city counts between the pairs of thresholds tested (%%g_unity_open%%/%%g_unity_c1%%, %%g_city_open%%/%%g_city_closed%%); human against human (`THVHBatPols`); more than one box in a turn; what a Yes does when the loser is the AI and the human the winner beyond the %%npairs_win%% D-WIN pairs (relation %%rel_yes_ans%%, no other non-news field differs).
- The **volatile words** at `%%vol_a%%`/`%%vol_b%%` were not identified; they are excluded from the identity proof.
