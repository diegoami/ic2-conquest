### I. AI nations' quarter income `[P][code]`

The staged week-11 start save (no staging beyond the week word) opened four times in one process, one End turn each (`g4_checks-I-20261012-110121.jsonl`, `quarter_round`; each opening is a fresh RNG draw, but the AI's decisions are almost deterministic here, so the four openings repeat the same figures for most nations: treat them as **one set of 15 AI nation-quarters plus Rome, not 60 independent samples**). `analysis_I.py` (-> `analysis_I.v4.txt`; `.txt`, `.v2`, `.v3` are earlier versions of the script kept) recomputes each nation's quarter from the code (`FUN_00451b40` 54741-54889: ships x 3, regular units `(troops div 200) x price`, recruit queue likewise, then `tax_base x tax div 100 + treasury + tax_base div 4 - 7 x cities - wealth div 20000 + trade`, trade = tax bases of partners and allies / 12, `FUN_004499ec`) from the after state, the unit prices read from memory, and compares: **Rome: predicted 2,221 = observed in all four openings** (the g3 +21; calibration of the script). **AI nations with no observable AI action: Seleucid, Macedonia, Numidia and Illyria predicted exactly** in every opening. Nations whose seat also spent money in the same End turn (money moved into an army purse by the AI, new recruit-queue slots, new fleets) match exactly once that spending is added as an estimate (Ptolemaic, Media, Thracia: exactly 500 into an army purse each; Bithynia: five new queue slots): **8 of 15 AI nations exact in all, 7 inexact**, by -273 (Carthage: a purse transfer of 997 and a changed unit list), +12 (Gaul), +28 (Greece), +11..+42 and -12 once (Celtiberia), -46 (Dacia), +34..+66 (Galatia), +23 (Armenia): residuals of the size of one trade partner's contribution (tax base / 12) or of an unmodelled seat action, not explained here. ### J. Why Greece's relations drop to -8 `[P][code]`

`[code]` the -8 is the cooldown the relation setter `FUN_00449b40` (48472-48546) writes when it is called with 0 on a trade (1 -> -8, alliance 2 -> -24, war 3 -> -18). It is called with 0 (a) by the AI seat's **partner swap**, the last loop of `FUN_0044fb7c` (53436-53449): for a trade partner `k` of the AI nation `x` (relation 1) and a candidate `c` (relation 0 with `x`, unity > 0, human flag 0, fewer than 3 partners) with `tax_base[k] < tax_base[c]`, `setter(x,k,0)` (so `k` gets -8) and `setter(x,c,1)` (53444-53445); the loop does not test that `k` is alive, and the loop before it (53420-53428) forms a trade with any nation of relation 0 when both have fewer than 3 partners; (b) by the transfer or conquest of a nation's last city (`FUN_0044bed8`, `FUN_0044c528`); (c) by `FUN_0044c360`, which first writes the reborn nation's own relation row to 0 directly (50548, not through the setter, hence one-sided) and then -8 with each moved city's owner (50558). `[P]` week 11, one End turn each, one process (`g4_checks-J-20261012-110249.jsonl`; `analysis_J.v2.txt` compares every relation pair of the staged file and the kept autosave): control without staging (twice): the swap produces natural -8s with **no Greece involved** (Ptolemaic-Dacia 1 -> -8, Celtiberia-Dacia, Illyria-Armenia, ...). **Dead Greece (L1: unity 0, capital -1, its 21 cities given to Macedonia) with no, or seven, disloyal Greek-allegiance cities**: Seleucid-Greece and Macedonia-Greece 1 -> -8 appear: the AI seats of Seleucid and Macedonia drop their trade with the dead Greece, whose tax base is 0, for a richer candidate (Armenia 432, Galatia 172); `analysis_J.v2.txt` lists the candidates. **Nine cities (rebirth fires)**: Rome-Greece 0 -> -6 (the rebirth's -8 after the tick's thaw), and Greece-Illyria 0 -> -8: the reborn Greece (an AI seat) formed a trade with Illyria and dropped it again in the same turn for Numidia or Armenia. **The rebirth leaves the relation matrix asymmetric**: afterwards Carthage, Seleucid and Macedonia still hold relation 1 with Greece while Greece's own row reads 0 (3 asymmetric pairs in the dead-nine and the alive-nine cases, none before, none in the controls; `[code]` 50548 writes only Greece's row), which is the "Carthage/Seleucid/Macedonia 1 -> 0" of the g3 table. All 191 relation changes of the six rounds are classified without a leftover: 114 thaw steps (negatives rise each week, up to twice per pair), 38 swaps, 37 trades formed, 2 rebirth cooldowns, 0 unexplained (`analysis_J.v2.txt`, last line). So the "Greece drops to -8" of the g3 staging is the AI's trade-partner swap acting on a dead or newly born nation, not a rebirth effect on the relations. Limit: the classification (a candidate with a higher tax base exists) is necessary, not a unique prediction of which partner is dropped; the rebirth runs are one trial each.
